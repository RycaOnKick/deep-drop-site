Privacy Policy

Deep Drop by Tidewater Games · Effective October 19, 2026

Deep Drop is a puzzle game made by Tidewater Games ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Deep Drop on iPhone or Android.

In short: we don't collect your personal information. Your progress stays on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. Deep Drop doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal details, and there's no Deep Drop account to sign up for. We don't run any servers, so the game doesn't send information to us.

Your game data stays on your device

Your scores, deepest dive, saved game and settings are stored only on your phone. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

If your phone's own backup is turned on (iCloud on iPhone, or Google backup on Android), this game data may be included in that backup. Apple or Google handle those backups under their own privacy policies.

Game Center leaderboards (iPhone)

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, Deep Drop posts your high score and deepest dive to Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards, together with the Game Center nickname you chose. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your Apple Account details. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your iPhone's Settings app, and Deep Drop works the same without it.

No ads, analytics or tracking

This version of Deep Drop contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Deep Drop is suitable for all ages. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Deep Drop changes how information is handled, for example by adding ads, we'll update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
